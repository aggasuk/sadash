Caveats for USD
  • 5 cycles spanning 30+ years and very different macro regimes (Feb-94 bond rout, Jun-99 post-LTCM, Jun-04 housing-boom liftoff, Dec-15 post-GFC ZIRP exit, Mar-22 post-COVID inflation). Decent N but heterogeneous.
  • Cash uses USSW (3M LIBOR-discounted swaps) for deep history; forwards use S0490FS (SOFR). Pre-2022 LIBOR-OIS basis (~10-25bp avg) means absolute carry numbers can be biased; relative cycle behaviour and z-scores remain informative.
  • Forward-spread trades (6M6M, 1Y1Y etc.) only have history back to ~2018 from the SOFR forward family, so those trades effectively rely on Mar-22 alone -- read the 'fwd' rows with low conviction.
