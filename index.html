> Momentum Dashboard

Capitulation/exhaustion signals identify the zone (fire at the low, often too early); price confirmations time the entry (fire after the low, low drawdown). The arm-and-confirm composite arms when an index's capitulation score crosses its threshold, then enters only when price reclaims its 50-day average within a window. Whether to wait is a property of the market, not a universal rule — so each index runs its own backtested mode (below). Edge = forward return in excess of the unconditional baseline; MAE = avg worst drawdown in the 3M after entry.

> Curve Regressions

5y5y vs 1y1y cross-currency regressions — for every pair (CCY1, CCY2) of {AUD, NZD, EUR, GBP, USD, KRW}, fit (CCY1 5y5y − CCY2 5y5y) = a + b · (CCY1 1y1y − CCY2 1y1y) on a 2Y daily window of BBG forwards. Positive z > +1.5 = 5y5y RICH (rec CCY1 / pay CCY2 5y5y). Negative z < −1.5 = 5y5y CHEAP (pay CCY1 / rec CCY2 5y5y). Sorted by |z| (largest dislocations first).

> Carry & Rolldown

3M carry & roll as Δ structure level (bp), curve unchanged: green + = level rises → payer / steepener / fly-payer earns; red − = receiver side earns. Curves = longer−shorter · flies = 2·belly−wings. Ratio = total ÷ 1y realized vol (3m-scaled). Carry %ile = today's total vs 5y history. Lvl 3m/1y = where the structure's level sits vs its own recent range (percentile; z + range on hover) — low = flat/tight, high = steep/wide. Score = carry ratio tilted by value: rewards positive carry entered cheap (payer at a low level %ile, receiver at a high one). ≤10/≥90 %ile highlighted. Fwd-structure history is par-reconstructed (approx). † = AUD roll path crosses the 2.5y OIS/BBSW split (~20bp basis) — excluded from Top Opportunities.
